# Sales Automation Workshop — 19 Nov 2026

**Participants:** Johannes (facilitator), Marieke (decider), Paul, Mahshad (growth/sales). Kimmo could not join.
**Format:** discovery alignment — optimistic hat, pessimistic hat, dot voting, workflow walkthrough, target selection.

## Optimistic hat — where are we in 6–12 months if this goes well?

- **Marieke:** overachieve on the #1 sales goal, ACV signed for H1, with a healthy pipeline to overachieve
  in H2. Requires accurate, predictable data, and investing in it now before it gets messy.
- **Mahshad:** leads, deals and account statuses update automatically; 20–50 net-new or stale accounts to
  reach out to weekly (~100 leads/week/person); automated nurture, follow-ups, reminders without errors.
- **Paul:** two funnels. Demand (pipeline generation, faster) and the sales funnel (standardised,
  auto-populated pricing and proposal templates) so time goes to talking to prospects.
- **Johannes (pre-workshop draft):** cut sales-funnel time by 50% by automating manual tasks and
  consolidating tools. Raised the question: are we optimising efficiency or increasing topline?

**Decision:** topline first over efficiency. Efficiency gains will be learned along the way.

**Draft goal:** *Solve the top three bottlenecks for sales in order to sign the target ACV.*

Pipeline math (Paul's calculation): prospect 200 companies/week, add 30 companies/week, 3 new meetings/week,
9 SQLs/month. H1 target ~€2.1M ACV, roughly 21 companies.

## Pessimistic hat — why would we fail? (dot-voted hotspots)

1. **Changing tools/CRM at the wrong time.** We'd build automations twice or for the wrong tool. Trialling
   tools takes hours; data migration risks data loss. Marieke and Kimmo leaning towards staying on HubSpot.
2. **Wrong timing / missed signals.** Reaching the right company at the wrong moment and losing them to a
   competitor. Harder to win back than a fresh conversation. Objectives can shift within a month.
3. **Limited or no ROI.** Set clear goals upfront; everything must link back to company goals.
4. **Top of funnel forgotten.** Becoming efficient but not booking more meetings.
5. *(Raised after voting by Marieke)* **No agreed market size.** Research has landed on 500, 4,000 or 8,000
   companies. We need an aligned TAM and SAM and a way to add companies weekly into outreach.

  positive feedback on the last three). Paul: keep it on the board as a pointer.
