# Discovery interview — Paul (17 Nov 2026)

Paul joined recently and brings fresh eyes.

## Funnel and HubSpot
- Prospects go through Chrono sequences (email, calls, LinkedIn). Once a meeting is booked they move to
  HubSpot: **pre-qualification → qualification → discovery → demo → proposal/negotiation → closed.**
- Stage movement and forecasting amounts are **fully manual** → human error risk.
- Claude job: difficulty is bubbling up the right companies at the right time; not perfected.
- Hasn't set up HubSpot workflows for inbound yet; expects inbound to largely run itself once done.

## Amplemarket (trial)
- Daily signals (e.g. company or person in the news) → start a sequence in one click; messaging and tasks
  automated except calls, which land in a task list.
- **Line drawn:** everything before a meeting is booked lives in the outreach tool; after, in the CRM.
- "Duo Copilot" for signals; contact search driven by Settly's value prop and customers; syncs with HubSpot.
- Has analytics (accounts reached, leads, meetings booked) for the prospecting side.
- Vendor says Sales Navigator could be dropped. ~€600/mo vs Chrono ~€200/mo, but consolidates tools.
- Johannes's view: don't rebuild this (scraping, signal DBs, filtering at scale) — buy it; integrations
  could break otherwise. Paul tried rebuilding and found it hard.
- **Decision timing:** ~end of November for both Chrono vs Amplemarket and HubSpot vs alternatives.
  CRM migration would take ~4 weeks; Paul sees the outreach tool as the bigger issue.
- Claude integration offered by Amplemarket — Paul couldn't set it up; asked Johannes to look
  (Settings → Integrations).

## Insights wanted
- Consolidate tools: jumping between tools loses data and speed.
- **Trace back from closed-won:** which signal and message led to the deal; find a common denominator
  across a very heterogeneous customer base (sizes, industries) — pain point, compliance issue, etc.
- **Closed-lost (102 accounts):** when to re-engage; older records have little info. He built a closed-lost
  Excel manually; uses Claude to dig through transcripts.
- **Nurture (~120 accounts):** hard to keep an eye on; when do they signal again?

## Pricing (his #1, "priority one, two and three")
- Not one-size-fits-all; many service levels → proposals slow, negotiation questions tedious.
- No single source of truth; Claude pulls one-off facts from Slack/emails as if they were standard.
- Built a calculator (package + services → price → push ACV to HubSpot), but no confidence in output;
  Erika, Paul and Marieke review → about a week.
- Wants: defined service tiers, a trusted pricing data source, proposal deck auto-populated from the call.
- *Status:* parked for Radar — central pricing source of truth coming from Kimmo/Erika.

## Other
- Ops handover: past feedback was "not enough info" (service level, needs) → ops redid discovery.
- Forecast vs actualised revenue isn't visible; would need checking after ~a year due to usage ramp.
- Demo prep is fine (~15 min, Mahshad).

- Paul shared the calculator and an Amplemarket invite.
